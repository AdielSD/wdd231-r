About me

My name is Sephora, and I’m from Peru. I’m someone who enjoys learning new things and trying different experiences that help me grow as a person. Right now I’m interested in software development, languages, and web design, and I like finding creative ways to improve my skills little by little. I also teach English, which has helped me become more patient and communicative. In my free time, I enjoy dancing, going to the gym, listening to music, and spending time with friends. I’ve also become interested in activities like surfing and motorcycles because I like trying things that feel exciting and different. I would describe myself as curious, friendly, and open minded. I enjoy meaningful conversations, meeting people from different backgrounds, and learning about new cultures and ideas. Even though I like having goals and thinking about my future, I also try to enjoy simple things and keep a balanced life. I’m currently focused on growing both personally and professionally while continuing to explore new hobbies and opportunities that interest me.
